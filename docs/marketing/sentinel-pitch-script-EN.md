# Sentinel IoT Platform — Pitch Script (English)
## Speaker Notes Style | 5 Minutes (11 slides)
### Thitipong Roongprasert | Pre-Seed $300K

---

> **Before you use this script**
> - Total time: **5 minutes** = ~27 seconds per slide
> - Read to understand, don't memorize — speak in your own words
> - Slow down on every number — let it land
> - Pause 1 second after each slide before clicking next

---

## SLIDE 1 — Title

> Introduce yourself before the first slide

- "Hi, I'm Thitipong Roongprasert, founder of Sentinel IoT Platform."
- "Sentinel is a production-grade industrial IoT monitoring system — ready to deploy today."
- "We're raising $300,000 at Pre-Seed."

---

## SLIDE 2 — Problem

> Open with the story — not the data

- "In 2021, a Singapore hospital lost **$2.3 million** worth of vaccines — because a fridge broke at 3am and nobody knew."
- "This still happens every single day across food, pharma, and industrial operations."
- "Three core problems: no real-time visibility, no automated alerts, and no audit trail."
- "One cold chain incident costs **$100,000 to $1 million or more.**"

---

## SLIDE 3 — Solution

> Don't list features — explain how it solves the problem

- "Sentinel monitors sensors in real-time every **5 seconds.**"
- "The moment a threshold is crossed, alerts fire automatically via Slack, LINE Notify, or Webhook."
- "It scales from **50 to 100,000+ devices** on the same architecture."
- "Every log is stored automatically — fully ready for FDA FSMA and EU Food Safety audits."

---

## SLIDE 4 — Market & ICP

> Move through TAM → SOM quickly — emphasize the beachhead

- "The global IoT monitoring market is **$53.8 billion** in 2026."
- "We're not targeting all of it — we start with a focused **$41M beachhead** in Southeast Asia."
- "Our priority ICP is **Food & Pharma Cold Chain** — where one incident costs $100K to $1M+."
- "SOM is calculated from 500 SME accounts at $522 per month."

---

## SLIDE 5 — Go-to-Market

> Walk through 3 phases quickly — anchor on Phase 1

- "Our GTM is simple: **Founder-led direct sales.**"
- "Phase 1, months 1–6: land **3 unpaid design partners** in Food Cold Chain in exchange for case studies."
- "Phase 2, months 7–12: convert to paying customers, targeting **$3,600 MRR.**"
- "Phase 3, months 13–18: 20 customers, **$12,000 MRR,** and our first Sales/BD hire."

---

## SLIDE 6 — Business Model

> Make Year 1 crystal clear — don't dwell on Year 2–3

- "Our model is deliberate: **Year 1 — Base SaaS only.**"
- "Starting at **$522/month** for 200 devices — **77% gross margin,** matching Samsara."
- "Once customers are paying, Year 2 unlocks Data Retention, Alert Pro, and Pro API upsells."
- "Enterprise and OEM come in Year 3 — only after PMF is clear."

---

## SLIDE 7 — Competitive Advantage

> Don't cover every competitor — pick the 2–3 most compelling

- "AWS IoT Core and Azure IoT Hub are solid infrastructure — **but you build everything yourself.**"
- "Sentinel is a **ready-to-deploy full-stack platform** — no engineering team needed."
- "We price by device count, not payload — **predictable costs** for SME buyers."
- "And we're the only platform with **LINE Notify integration** — built specifically for SEA."

---

## SLIDE 8 — Technical Credibility

> This is your strongest differentiator — slow down and let it land

- "I want to prove this isn't a prototype — with real numbers."
- "**107 automated tests — 100% pass rate,** tested May 12th, 2026."
- "Covering Unit, Integration, and **Security tests** including JWT forgery, SQL injection, and multi-tenant isolation."
- "Load tested at **1,003 requests per second** — p95 latency of **112ms,** well under our 200ms SLO."

---

## SLIDE 9 — Financials

> Emphasize gross margin and benchmark — don't read every projection number

- "We're targeting **77% gross margin** — matching Samsara, the global IoT SaaS leader."

- "Year 3 projection: **60 customers, $594K ARR** — conservative case."
